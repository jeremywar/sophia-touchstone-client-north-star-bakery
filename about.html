About North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery is a family-friendly neighborhood bakery that has served our community since 2010. We bake fresh breads, pastries, cookies, and cakes for everyday cravings, special celebrations, and everything in between. Whether you're a longtime regular, a family planning an event, or a traveler looking for a good place to stop, we hope you'll feel right at home the moment you walk through our door.

Story of the bakery

North Star Bakery opened its doors in 2010 with a simple goal: to bake the kind of food that brings people together. What began as a small shop built on a handful of family recipes quickly grew into something more. It became a gathering place for neighbors, a first stop for early risers, and a welcome surprise for travelers who pulled off the road looking for something warm and homemade.

Over the years, our community has grown right alongside us. We've baked birthday cakes for kids who now stop in on their way home from college, supplied cookie trays for school fundraisers and community suppers, and filled dessert tables for weddings, graduations, and holiday gatherings. Our menu has expanded and our team has grown, but the heart of the bakery hasn't changed. We still bake from scratch, greet regulars by name, and believe a fresh pastry can turn an ordinary day into a better one. North Star has always been as much about community as it is about baking.

Sourcing philosophy

We believe great baking starts with great ingredients. Whenever possible, we partner with local farms and suppliers for our eggs, dairy, flour, and seasonal fruit, which keeps our flavors fresh and our dollars close to home. We choose quality over shortcuts, skip unnecessary additives, and let the seasons guide our specials. When you taste something from our case, you're tasting care that begins long before it reaches our kitchen.

Staff highlights

The people behind the counter are what make North Star feel like home. Our bakers arrive before sunrise to shape dough and pull the first trays from the oven, while our front counter team often knows a regular's order before they reach the register. Many have been with us for years, and each new face brings fresh ideas to the menu. Stop in and meet the folks who bake your favorites.
