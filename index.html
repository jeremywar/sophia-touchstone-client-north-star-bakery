What we’re known for

Since 2010, North Star Bakery has been a warm, familiar stop for neighbors, families, and travelers passing through. We're known for fresh-from-the-oven breads, pastries, and treats made from scratch every morning, along with custom baked goods that make birthdays, weddings, and community events a little sweeter. Whether you're grabbing a coffee and a cinnamon roll for the road or ordering for a crowd, you'll always find a friendly face and something delicious waiting.
